Applies to the material in this folder, i.e. data/letters/ (the sample letters),
data/reference.json (the reference metadata), the extracted values in data/lookups.json and
prompts/ (prompts, encoding rules, few-shot examples).

Source of the letters (transcriptions) and of the reference metadata:
Höflechner, Walter; Scholger, Martina; Wagner, Alexandra; Strutz, Sabrina; Steiner, Elisabeth
(eds.) 2025: Joseph von Hammer-Purgstall. Korrespondenz. Graz.
https://gams.uni-graz.at/hpe, hdl.handle.net/11471/559.20 (GAMS 559.20).

Full legal text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/legalcode
